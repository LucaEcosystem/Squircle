Spacing

One scale, three relationships: within components, between groups, between sections.

Within components

Padding and gaps inside a component come from the small end of the scale — 2xs through sm. Buttons, fields, and menu items never invent their own. 

.sq-stack {
  display: grid;
  gap: var(--sq-space-md);
}

Between groups

Related groups separate at md to lg. Cards in a listing, fields in a form, items in a menu — close enough to belong, far enough to scan.

md — 1.5rem
Default group separation: stacks, grids, card content.
lg — 2rem
Emphatic separation: panels from neighbors, sections within pages.

Between sections

Major page sections separate at the fluid section token — pages scroll, content breathes, nobody measures by hand.

When may I break the scale?

When a component justifies its own density in its documentation — and almost never elsewhere.
