The 64-pixel rule

Section rhythm should be a token, not a feeling — and what happens when every team invents its own.

Reviewed for accuracy

Every team invents its own

Ask five teams how far apart two sections sit and you get five answers, three of them "it depends". The page pays for every disagreement in a currency readers feel but cannot name: unease. Rhythm is infrastructure. Tokenize it the way you tokenize color, and the arguments end — the way arguments about the brand blue ended years ago.

Whitespace establishes hierarchy — it never merely fills gaps.

Our rule is simple: sections breathe at a fluid clamp(4rem, 8vw, 7rem). Narrow screens get the minimum, wide screens the maximum, and nobody measures anything by hand. The full scale lives in the spacing documentation.

Questions we still get

Doesn't all that space waste the fold?

The fold is not a container to fill. Readers scroll; what they will not do is decode a wall.

What about data-dense screens?

Density belongs inside components (tables, rows), never between sections. Group tightly, separate generously.
